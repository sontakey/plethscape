# Plethscape product brief

Confirmed scope: a ChatPPG educational product for a general audience,
with optional depth for science and engineering learners. Plethscape retains
its standalone application build and is served at chatppg.com/plethscape.

The core experience is an anatomically grounded human model, selectable sensing
sites, age and heart-rate controls, and convincing simulated PPG. Prioritize the
human and heart's visual realism, with readable organ colors and a clear body
outline. Use the ChatPPG paper/ink/blue light theme by default, with DM Sans,
readable scientific plots and a compact ChatPPG/Plethscape product header.
An explicit Appearance choice retains the dark workspace as a saved option.
Keep the blue/amber anatomical outline controls and all simulation behavior.
Scientific colors remain distinct from brand/action colors; infrared uses
explicit violet false color because infrared itself is invisible.

Explain waveform changes in plain language first. Keep the dicrotic notch,
systolic peak, reflected wave, sampling information, and modeling assumptions
available for deeper exploration. Geometry provenance and simulated physiology
must remain distinguishable.

Breathing stays part of the primary physiology view. Walking, running, and
accelerometry are secondary; improvements to those should follow the human,
heart, sensing interaction, and PPG experience.
